Download Gachara

Version 0.19.0, published on 14 October 2026. Windows, Android and the browser run the same Gachara and are always released together. It's free, with no ads.

Windows

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11. Run the installer; it isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen asks once: "More info" → "Run anyway".

Updates by itself: once installed, it downloads new versions and asks you to restart.

For phone-only games (Dokkan Battle): Gachara reads them from your phone shown in a window on the PC. For Android everything is in this download: it includes scrcpy (free and open source), and a button in Gachara opens it. For an iPhone, also install 3uAirPlayer from its official site: it isn't ours to redistribute.

Android

Download for Android

Android 7 or later. Open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks (it isn't on the Play Store).

Updates by itself: it downloads its new pages and restarts in a second. When a new APK is needed it installs it for you (Android asks once to allow Gachara to install updates). Your data stays.

In your browser

Open Gachara

Nothing to install, on any device, iPhone included: to keep it like an app, use "Add to Home Screen" from your browser's menu.

Always the latest version. A browser can't read your box from the game: sign in with Google to bring your progress from the Windows or Android app, or mark what you own by hand.

iPhone and iPad

Gachara for iPhone and iPad (app version 0.19.0, iOS 15 or later) isn't on the App Store: that needs a paid developer account, and Gachara is a free hobby project. You install it yourself, free, with your own Apple ID:

  1. Install AltStore (it uses AltServer on your PC or Mac) or SideStore (it works from the phone alone after the first setup).
  2. Add the Gachara source: Add to AltStore or paste https://axel3614.github.io/gachara/altstore.json in "Sources".
  3. Install Gachara from that source. Or download the app file (IPA) and open it with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.

Every 7 days the free Apple ID signature expires and has to be refreshed: AltStore does it by itself when your PC with AltServer is on the same Wi-Fi, SideStore on the phone. If it expires, Gachara won't open until you refresh it, but your box and settings stay. Deleting the app deletes them: sign in with Google and they come back.

Updates by itself: Gachara downloads its new pages and restarts in a second. When a new app is needed (rarely), AltStore or SideStore shows the update from the source.

Not yet on iPhone: reading your box from the game screen and the NIKKE official site inside the app. Until then, sign in with Google to bring your box from Gachara for Windows or Android, or mark it by hand.

The iPhone app is on its way. Until then, on an iPhone or iPad use Gachara in the browser and add it to your Home Screen.
