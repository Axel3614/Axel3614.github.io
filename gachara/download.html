Download Gachara

Version 0.18.1, published on 14 October 2026. Windows, Android and the browser run the same Gachara and are always released together. It's free, with no ads.

Windows

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11. Run the installer; it isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen asks once: "More info" → "Run anyway".

Updates by itself: once installed, it downloads new versions and asks you to restart.

For phone-only games (Dokkan Battle): Gachara reads them from your phone shown in a window on the PC. For Android everything is in this download: it includes scrcpy (free and open source), and a button in Gachara opens it. For an iPhone, also install 3uAirPlayer from its official site: it isn't ours to redistribute.

Android

Download for Android

Android 7 or later. Open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks (it isn't on the Play Store).

Tells you when to update: when a new version is out the app shows a button; download it and install it over the old one, your data stays.

In your browser

Open Gachara

Nothing to install, on any device, iPhone included: to keep it like an app, use "Add to Home Screen" from your browser's menu.

Always the latest version. A browser can't read your box from the game: sign in with Google to bring your progress from the Windows or Android app, or mark what you own by hand.

iPhone and iPad

There is no iOS app: Apple only lets apps be installed from its App Store, which needs a paid developer account, and Gachara is a free hobby project. On an iPhone or iPad, use Gachara in the browser and add it to your Home Screen.
