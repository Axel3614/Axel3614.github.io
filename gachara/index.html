Your gacha companion

Gachara is a free Windows app that runs next to your games. It builds one tier list from the best guide sites, keeps your own box up to date, and tells you which teams you can field and what to upgrade next, based on the characters you actually own.

Download for Windows Support it on Ko-fi

The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen asks for confirmation the first time: "More info" → "Run anyway". Installed copies update by themselves.

What it does

  • One tier list that blends the best guide sites, weighted by how reliable each one is for each kind of content.
  • Your box, kept up to date by itself: from the game's official website where there is one, or by reading the game window on your screen while you scroll your characters.
  • Teams and upgrades built on what you own: raid teams, event teams, what to level, which gear to farm.
  • An overlay above the game, daily checklists, pull planning and notifications for what is about to end.

Games

NIKKE, Dragon Ball Z Dokkan Battle and Chaos Zero Nightmare, with Brown Dust 2 coming next.

Free, and safe for your account

  • No ads and no account needed. Everything is stored on your PC.
  • Gachara only looks at what the game window shows, like a screenshot. It never reads the game's memory or its network traffic, and it never taps or plays for you.
  • When you sign in to an official game site inside Gachara, you sign in directly with that site: Gachara never sees or stores your password.
  • An optional account (Settings → Sign in with Google) keeps your progress the same on all your devices. See the privacy policy for exactly what it stores.
