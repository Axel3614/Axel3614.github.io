Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: Gachara works on your PC. Unless you choose to send a report or sign in, nothing about you leaves your computer. We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and we never read your games' memory or network traffic.

Who we are

Gachara is a free, independent hobby project made by Axel394 in Italy. For any question about your data, or to ask us to see or delete it, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public: don't write personal details there, just tell us what you need and we'll reply on how to proceed privately.

What stays on your PC

Everything Gachara needs to work is stored in its folder on your computer and is never sent to us:

  • your box (the characters you own and their levels, gear and other details), your settings, checklists, pull numbers and summon history;
  • the guide data Gachara downloads, and screen captures you take with it;
  • if you use Gachara in Italian, the translations of guide and news texts: they are made on your PC by a translation engine that comes with the app (the one Firefox uses), and no text is sent anywhere to be translated;
  • the sessions of the official game websites you sign in to inside Gachara (for example Blablalink for NIKKE). You sign in directly with those sites: Gachara never sees or stores your password, and it only reads what the page shows you.

Uninstalling Gachara and deleting its folder (Settings → "Open the Gachara data folder") removes all of it.

Reading the game screen

If you turn on Screen Reader, Gachara looks at the game window you choose, every few seconds, to read your box and similar screens. The images are processed on your PC and are never stored online or sent anywhere. Gachara doesn't record other windows, doesn't read the game's memory or network traffic, and never taps or plays for you.

Public websites Gachara visits

To keep guides and game data current, Gachara downloads public pages and images from guide and database sites (such as Prydwen, nikkers.cc, Loot & Waifus, DokkanStats, dokkaninfo, Nikke-db, BD2DB and Banner Recommendation) and the public news of the official game websites (such as the Brown Dust 2 news) and checks GitHub for app updates. Most days the game data comes as a ready-made package from the same public GitHub repository as the app (refreshed once a day), and the sites above are visited only when that package is missing or out of date. Like any visit from a browser, those sites see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. Gachara sends them nothing about you.

Reports you choose to send

The Feedback section lets you share how you cleared a stage, report a problem or suggest an idea. Nothing is sent until you press "Send". What is sent:

  • what you filled in (for example the team, items, number of attempts and your notes) and, if you add one, a screenshot;
  • the Gachara version and a random number created by your installation, used only to limit spam and to avoid counting the same player twice. It doesn't identify you.
  • for a problem report, unless you untick it, technical info to understand what went wrong: Windows version, screen size and scaling, app language, how many cards your box has, the app's own error messages of the last two days (with your Windows user name removed from file paths) and, if Screen Reader is on, its state (the window title only if you picked the window yourself) and, if you didn't add a screenshot, the picture of the game window it is reading (for Brown Dust 2 only the list of your costumes, never the character art). You can see all of it before sending.

Reports are stored in our database at Supabase, our database provider. Other players can see the team, items and attempts you shared; your free text is shown only after we review it; screenshots are never shown publicly. A screenshot may contain your in-game name: crop it if you prefer.

Optional account: Sign in with Google

Gachara works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, to keep your progress the same on all your devices:

  • What we receive from Google: your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture link (the basic "openid", "email" and "profile" permissions). We don't ask for, and can't access, your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What we use it for: only to recognise your account and keep your Gachara progress (box, checklists, cleared stages, pull numbers and settings) in sync between your devices. Website sessions and screen captures are never synced.
  • Where it is stored: in our database at Supabase, which handles the sign-in for us.
  • Deleting it: you can delete your account and all its synced data from Gachara's Settings at any time, or ask us to do it.

Gachara's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never use this information for advertising and never sell or transfer it to others.

How long we keep data

  • Data on your PC: until you delete it.
  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: until they are removed, by us or at your request.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or asking us. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can also complain to a data protection authority; in Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

We process account data and reports because you choose to use those features (your consent), and update checks because the app needs them to stay up to date.

Children

If you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in or sending reports.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and important changes will be announced in the app.
